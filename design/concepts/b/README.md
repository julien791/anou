# B · Postmark

**Idea.** Every day of the stay is a postcard home. Photographs live inside perforated stamp
and postcard frames on warm paper, and the copy always sits on paper, never on the photo.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| paper | `#F3ECDF` (with fine grain) | Page |
| card | `#FFFDF8` | Postcards, stamp frames |
| ink | `#1D1B22` | Text |
| penny | `#1F3C8C` | Blue Penny blue: logo, Passport cover, ticket stubs (9:1 on paper) |
| hibiscus | `#B8322A` | Postmarks, labels, the one action (5.4:1 on paper) |
| sea | `#2B6B63` | Price seals, done states |
| sun | `#E2A531` | Host avatar, small highlights |

## Type
DM Serif Display for headlines and place names, Oswald (condensed, tracked caps) for
stamp-style labels, Work Sans for reading. Headline 38–42 px.

## Photography
Balanced, around 45 % photo. Photos are framed objects: the hero is a large stamp, suggestions
are 4:5 postcards slightly rotated, Tonight items are tickets with a photo header and a
torn stub. Because text never overlays the photo, sunlight legibility is solved by
construction and any photo crop works.

## Motion and the conditions-matched hero
The clip plays inside a perforated stamp marked MAURITIUS. A red postmark then *thuds*
onto the corner (0.7 s, overshoot) carrying the place, date and today's condition word in
its centre ("Flat", "Windy"), with wavy cancellation lines. The postmark is the conditions
engine made visible: a dated, local, hand-applied proof that this is today. Reduced motion:
poster frame and the postmark already in place.

## Strengths and risks
+ Most ownable and most "Mauritian" (stamps, Blue Penny); connects directly to the
  Passport and makes stamps the brand's spine.
+ Best legibility in sun.
− Most hand-made detail to build and keep consistent; can slide into nostalgia or
  scrapbook if overdone. Smaller photos sell the sea less hard than A.
