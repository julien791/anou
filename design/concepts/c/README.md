# C · Sega

**Idea.** Today's condition is one huge word, and the clip plays inside its letters. Colour
blocks borrowed from sega skirts and painted shopfronts carry the rest; the type shouts so
the copy never has to.

## Type
Anton (condensed grotesque, caps) for the condition word, headlines and times; Manrope for
reading. The condition word runs at 150–196 px.

## Photography
Around 60 % photo. Photos sit in arches (a nod to colonial verandas and temple doorways)
and in 4:5 cards that end in a solid colour block where the text lives. On Tonight the
sunset photo fills the letters of TONIGHT.

## Motion and the conditions-matched hero
Two views of the same clip. At the top, the condition word ("FLAT", "WINDY") is a cut-out:
the clip plays inside the letters, which wipe in left to right. Below, the arch shows the
whole clip with a small wind-by-coast dial (island outline, wind arrows, your coast
highlighted). Glance at the word, then the place. Reduced motion: still poster in the
letters, no wipe.

## Strengths and risks
+ Most distinctive at a glance and most shareable; the word works as a widget, a
  notification image and a social card.
− Loudest; risks feeling like a festival poster rather than a calm friend, and the
  cut-out word needs a short, punchy condition vocabulary (Flat, Windy, Rain, Clear, Swell)
  that the conditions engine must supply. Condensed caps are less readable for longer
  headlines, so headlines must stay short.
