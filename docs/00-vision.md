# 00 · Vision

## The one-line pitch

Every other Mauritius app answers "what is there to see?". Anou answers "what should we do
today?", because in Mauritius that depends on conditions a visitor cannot read: which coast
the wind is hitting, whether the plateau is under cloud, whether the lagoon is flat enough
for the catamaran, which side the sunset is on.

## Who it is for

- **Tourists** on a 5 to 14 night stay, mostly couples and families, mostly on iPhone,
  arriving via a QR code at their hotel or Airbnb. They want one trustworthy nudge a day,
  not another directory.
- **Hosts** (hotels, guesthouses, Airbnb owners). They want fewer repetitive questions and a
  digital welcome guide that replaces the laminated binder.
- **Businesses** (activity operators, restaurants, bars, guides, drivers, artisans). They want
  tourists to find them at the moment the tourist is deciding, not weeks before.

## The reason to download

Two push notifications a day, written like a local friend:

- **Morning brief (~8:00):** what today is good for, given the conditions, with two or three
  concrete suggestions and one practical tip.
- **Tonight (~16:00):** what is on this evening near the guest.

Everything else (Passport, Kreol word, host page, directory) earns its place by making those
two messages better or by giving the guest a reason to open the app between them.

## What Anou is not

- Not a booking engine (v1 links out; bookings may come later).
- Not a map of everything. Curation over coverage.
- Not an ad channel. One sponsored item per brief, clearly marked, never more.

## Name

"Anou" is Kreol Morisien for "let's" ("Anou alé" = let's go). Tagline: *Anou. Let's go,
Mauritius.* Domain and trademark checks are an open task (see docs/05-business.md).

## Success for v1

A guest who scans at check-in opens the brief on at least 4 of their first 7 mornings, and
at least one partner business can see visits attributed to Anou.
