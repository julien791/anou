# 05 · Business model and go-to-market

## Revenue

| Tier | Who | Price (working assumption) | What they get |
|------|-----|---------------------------|---------------|
| Free listing | Any business | 0 | Name, photos, hours, one standing offer, appears in Explore |
| Pro | Operators, restaurants, venues | ~MUR 2,500/month | Eligible for brief and Tonight suggestions when conditions match, event posting, stamp station eligibility, stats dashboard |
| Boosts | Pro businesses | Per day, by region and slot | Sponsored slot in the morning brief or Tonight (max one per message), geofenced nudge |
| Stamp station | Pro businesses | Included in Pro, limited slots per stamp | Foot traffic in exchange for a small reward |
| Hotel plan | Hotels | Per property per month, later | Multi-unit, staff logins, own events, branded landing, guest stats |

Hosts (Airbnb, guesthouses) pay nothing. They are distribution.

## Distribution

1. Start with one region (west coast: Flic en Flac to Le Morne) and 20–30 Airbnbs plus 2–3
   hotels. Density matters more than coverage because the brief must have real options.
2. The pitch to hosts: replace your welcome binder, cut the questions, free.
3. The pitch to businesses: you only appear when today is a good day for you, and you can
   see who came.
4. Print: a QR card for the room, a counter sticker for stamp stations.
